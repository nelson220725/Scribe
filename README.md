## About The Project

### Missed Class? Forgot to make a study guide? Don't Worry! Get notes for all your classes with Scribe!

**Cornell App Dev Hack Challenge Spring 2024**

Scribe is a mobile application designed to revolutionize the way students share and access lecture notes and study guides. Whether you're looking to catch up on missed lectures or enhance your understanding with supplementary materials, Scribe has you covered. Join Scribe today and embark on a journey of collaborative learning and academic excellence!

### Key Features

📚 **Course-Based Organization** - Select your courses and access notes specific to each class

📤 **Easy Note Sharing** - Upload your notes as PDFs to share with classmates

📥 **Access Quality Notes** - Browse and download notes from other students in your courses

👥 **Cornell Community** - Built specifically for Cornell students to collaborate and succeed together

🔒 **Secure & Organized** - Your notes are safely stored and easily accessible whenever you need them

## Future Features

We have exciting plans to enhance Scribe with new features:

- [ ] **Comments and Likes** - Share your thoughts and appreciate great notes
- [ ] **Starred Notes** - Bookmark your favorite notes for quick access
- [ ] **Note Tags** - Organize by lecture, prelim, study guide, and more
- [ ] **User Karma System** - Recognize the best note-takers in the community
- [ ] **Advanced Search** - Find exactly what you need with improved filtering
- [ ] **Notification System** - Stay updated when new notes are uploaded to your courses

See the [open issues](https://github.com/nelson220725/Scribe/issues) for a full list of proposed features and known issues.
